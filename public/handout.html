What we do

Landex Systems reads your project documents and compares them to your site capture, including scans, videos, and walkthroughs. We give you back a list of every place the paperwork and the field do not match, with the photo evidence next to the governing document. Your team works the list.

  • Substitutions caught at install instead of at hydrotest.

How to start

We start on a small section of the project. One room, one module, one system. We work with the documents and the capture you already have. You see what we found and you decide whether it is worth expanding to the whole site.
